About

I design AI products people can trust, and today I lead product design at Zyvora Innovations in Bengaluru.

My path into product design started in research: an internship studying how digital campaigns actually reach people taught me to look at behaviour before opinions. From there I moved into designing AI products, first for computer-vision systems and then for wider AI platforms, where the hard part was never the model but helping people understand what it saw and decide what to do next.

As a senior product designer I learned to own a product end to end, from framing the problem to shipping the last state of the last screen. At Zyvora I now run product design across an AI-first portfolio, working between product management and design: setting direction, building the design system, and keeping every screen honest about what the data says.

I care about the moment when two screens disagree, because that is when people stop trusting a product. So I fix the data model before I draw anything, and I design for the person on shift at 3 a.m.: status is never colour alone, everything works from the keyboard, and nothing breaks when the network does.
